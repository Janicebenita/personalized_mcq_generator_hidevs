# BloomPath — Product Soul

## Our promise

**Learn from evidence. Grow with every question.**

BloomPath exists to turn a learner's own English study notes into understandable practice: a question, a defensible answer, a visible source, and a clear explanation of what comes next.

## Principles we do not compromise

1. **Evidence before fluency.** A polished AI question without source support is not good enough. Show the underlying note and distinguish generated wording from a source template.
2. **Honest adaptation.** Explain the actual two-correct-up, one-incorrect-down rule. Do not pretend to measure latent ability, cognitive traits, or calibrated difficulty.
3. **Learner agency.** Make it easy to review evidence, choose a response, reflect on confidence, and export work. The system supports learning; it does not label the learner.
4. **Privacy by architecture.** Favor on-device inference and no mandatory account. Describe network downloads accurately and never add telemetry without explicit product review and disclosure.
5. **Graceful degradation.** Source practice should remain useful without the AI model. If AI generation fails, say so; do not relabel a template as AI output.
6. **Accessibility is core.** Semantic controls, keyboard support, legible layouts, and reduced-motion preferences are requirements, not decorative extras.
7. **Truth over leaderboard optics.** Do not fabricate deployment status, test passes, educational effectiveness, citations, usage metrics, or performance benchmarks.
8. **Small, reviewable changes.** Prefer reliable improvements to dramatic rewrites near submission. Preserve existing user-facing behavior unless changes are tested.

## Voice and interaction

Use clear, encouraging, nonjudgmental language. Prefer “This answer differs from the source definition” over “You failed.” Explain what the learner can inspect or try next. Avoid exaggerated promises such as “guaranteed mastery,” “100% accurate AI,” or “scientifically diagnosed weakness.”

## Product boundaries

BloomPath currently focuses on English term–definition notes in `.txt`/`.md` or pasted text. It supports Recall, Connect and Challenge tasks, topic-level observations, confidence reflection, and JSON/CSV export. It does **not** claim arbitrary PDF/DOCX understanding, diagram reasoning, code execution, secure examination delivery, or validated educational diagnosis.

## Release integrity

Before public release, verify the model manifest and hashes, dependency and license notices, static build, test/evaluation artifacts, browser experience, responsive design, fallback behavior, and actual deployment URL. A placeholder demo link stays a placeholder until the deployment is verified.

## Decision rule for contributors and agents

When a change improves appearance but weakens evidence traceability, accessibility, truthful provenance, or graceful fallback, reject the change. When evidence is insufficient, surface the limitation rather than manufacture certainty.
